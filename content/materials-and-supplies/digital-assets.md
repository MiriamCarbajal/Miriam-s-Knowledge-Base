# Digital Assets
## With technology being such a heavy influence in everyones day-to-day, most of our memories our held in our devices.  Unless there is a big occasion that you would want to do a scrapbook for, the images will most likely stay on your device. Luckily with so many editing apps we have the pleasure of being able to make a scrapbook like image on our phones. We get the enjoyment of putting something fun together without having to get the images printed and buy the supplies if it's not your kind of thing. They make great and unique posts for social media. 

> It can actually be a little bit easier to make "scrapbook" pages on our phone since mistakes can be reversed vs on paper it's not as easy. 

### Types of Digital Scrapbooking Assets
* Digital Papers- You can find backgrounds for your page of all styles and colors. 
* Embellishments and Stickers- There's all sorts of "stickers" you can find online.
* Frame and Cutouts- There's various transparent frames that help add something unique to your page.
* Word Art and Typography- The accessibility to all of the fonts and being able to find banners and cool headers.
