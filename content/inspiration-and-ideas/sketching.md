# Sketching 

## Sketching is a great way to brainstorm for any project you do. It's a great way to add to your pages as well. Wether your making a digital page and want to sketch ideas or vise versa. The possibilities with sketching are endless. Sketching can save you time and help when it comes to the assembly phase of your scrapbook. 

> A sketch helps act as a blueprint for your page. Wether it gets done digitally or manually on paper

### Benefits of Pre-Sketching 
* Prevents cluttered pages by establishing clear visual of your vision and ideas. 
* Helps you test alignment with your [[moodboards]] design direction. 
* Makes it easier to decide where internal links and interactive page tags should live across your project.
* Helps you decide what works for you in terms of stickers, color, frames, fonts.
* Good way to save ideas for future pages you will make.
