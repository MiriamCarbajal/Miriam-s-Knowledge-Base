# Scrapbook Layout Themes

## A good layout makes sure that everything feels cohesive and clean. It's not necessary to follow a layout but it helps get ideas for ways to make your page your own. Sometimes you can run into a layout that feels like a good representative of the memory you are trying to portray. You have the option of what you want to tell with your page. 

> There's times you can just grab something from various layouts to grab inspiration and make it your own. 

### Popular Layouts
1.  Grid Layouts- Multiple photos organized into uniform boxes.
2. Single Photo- One large image is highlighted with some area around it for other details.
3. Double Page Spread- Using two pages in order to fit your desired art.
4. Collage Style- Many overlapping images and heavy imagery. Ofter pairing well with [[moodboards]].
5. Journal Spread- These pages are centered more around a text.
