# Moodboards

## Mood boards help organize any ideas that you have in mind and allow you to play around with what you might want to add to your page.  A mood board is seen as a template, and it doesn't have to be perfect because it's not your finalized page. Mood boards are actually used very often in fields like desginers, film, and for company branding.

>Moodboards can be made for all occasions, at any time, and for any project. There's various tools online that can help create a good moodboard if you're stuck.

### Tips of What to Include in Your Moodboard
* Colors- Have an idea of what colors or shades you would like. The amount of shades or colors is up to you. [[color-palettes]]
* Fonts- Font help set a tone to your moodboard and they can sometimes be overlooked but the proper font changes the feeling you want portrayed. 
* Photography- These can be your personal images or images you find online that you find inspiring.
